Graduate Climate Fellowship 2027

The Example Climate Institute invites applications for its Graduate Climate Fellowship, a twelve-week paid program for students pursuing a master's degree in environmental science, public policy, economics, or a related field.

Eligibility

  • Currently enrolled in a master's program
  • Strong written communication skills
  • Interest in climate adaptation policy

Key dates

Applications close on 15 November 2026.
Interviews take place in December.

Fellows receive a monthly stipend of 2,000 EUR and travel support. The program is based in Lisbon, Portugal, with some remote work possible.
